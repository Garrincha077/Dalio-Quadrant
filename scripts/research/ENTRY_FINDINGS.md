# Entry opportunity findings · 26 October 2026

The question is which conditions preceded the greatest return **above each asset’s own usual return**. Winners vary by holding horizon, period and condition detail. These are retrospective associations, with revised macro data; no confirmed prospective entry edge is established.

All means below are cumulative nominal USD, net of assumed 10 bp on each entry and exit. Lift is a difference in percentage points, not annualised alpha. Each horizon uses its own complete common-date cohort.

## What can be concluded

For actual ETFs at 12 months, the signed-state leaders are: SPY below-trend activity / inflation below 2%; IEF and GLD below-trend activity / inflation above 2%; TIP below-trend activity / inflation below 2%; DBC above-trend activity / inflation above 2%. At 3 and 36 months some leaders change. These are the best observed conditional lifts in this sample, not universal entry rules.

Long history does not simply confirm the ETF results. In particular, long gold’s leading 12M state is above-trend activity / inflation above 2%, whereas GLD’s is below-trend activity / inflation above 2%. The same-date proxy check in the app helps separate period effects from different return constructions.

Direction can produce large historical lifts, especially for long gold and commodities when activity and inflation both rise. Such rankings use many fewer cases and remain unconfirmed after correcting the scan’s adequately repeated comparisons with Holm. Some ETF direction-family winners have negative lift because the other direction cells do not meet the fixed repetition requirement; do not treat those rows as positive entry opportunities.

No adequately repeated positive cell passes the panel-wide Holm 5% threshold. Positive unadjusted intervals alone are not enough after scanning 4,804 combinations. Episode counts, non-overlapping starts, decade-matched lift and leave-one-episode diagnostics are provided beside the results.

The methodology audit corrected mixed-reference-month live quadrants and numerical anchor equality. The existing frozen historical source changes zero classifications. The economic anchors were not fitted to return winners.
